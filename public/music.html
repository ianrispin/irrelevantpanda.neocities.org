I LOVE all kinds of music and I'm always on the lookout for new stuff. I currently have just over 350 curated playlists, all sorted into folders and subfolders because I'm super normal about categorizing things.

I switched from spotify to apple music earlier this year, and there were 3 main reasons for this - 1. My family already pays for apple's all-in-one subscription, so we were technically already paying for apple music; 2. I don't like some of the choices spotify's ceo has been making, namely putting up ICE recruitment ads and investing in AI drone tech for the military; 3. I learned that spotify is one of the lowest in the industry in terms of artist payouts. Please note, none of this is meant to shame people who do use spotify, I understand that not everyone has an iPhone, so this isn't a viable switch for everybody. This is just the decision that I came to based on my feelings and situation.

You can find all of my current, updated playlists on my Apple Music. Most of my playlists are also on my Spotify, they just aren't fully updated.

On this page, I list and talk about some of my favorite artists, albums, and playlists that I've made.

Albums

Here's some of my favorite albums:

Mia Gargaret by Gia Margaret

This is one of my top 2 favorite albums of all time, and was solidly my number one for like a year after I heard it. It's almost entirely instrumental, with ambient synths that are grounded with gorgeous piano and guitar melodies. Gia actually lost her voice in late 2019 - this album was released in early 2020. It was kind of her way of finding herself as an artist through that difficulty. We actually hear several clips of her doing vocal training throughout the album, and at the final song, we finally hear her sing.


It's Never Fair, Always True by JAWNY

This is my other favorite album, and by far my favorite indie pop album. The song transitions are incredible (I love it when artists pay attention to song transitions in their album). The melodies are fantastic and catchy. The storytelling is amazing too, this might also be my favorite breakup album.


you made me / skin on linen by PISS

Intense, insane, and amazing punk/noise rock album. Opening song eases you into the album, and then just goes right into the noise. Heavy themes of rape, trauma, and misogyny. Very very good album, and one of my favorites released in 2026.


bbno$ by bbno$

One of my favorite rap albums, every single song on here is such a bop.


Getting Killed by Geese

Amazing rock album, one of my favorite albums of 2025. It starts off so hard, and the flow of the whole album is amazing. So many incredible songs, and no bad ones.
